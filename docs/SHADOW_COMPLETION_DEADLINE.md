## I propose a private, timestamped completion record

I keep the same selected shadows, ten-second budget, child alarm, publication guard and failure statuses. I move the child's final stream flush before successful completion publication, so pending report/output work cannot occur after the success record. Failure to flush or read the monotonic clock refuses completion. The child samples `CLOCK_MONOTONIC`, writes one fixed-size record containing an explicit success tag and the timestamp, closes the pipe and immediately calls `_exit`. The record stays below `PIPE_BUF`; this one writer and initially empty pipe have no variable-length framing. Failed or incomplete writes refuse success. No program or callback executes after the record.

The parent retains its existing monotonic start and exact deadline calculation. After reaping it requires a complete valid record, a nonnegative well-formed timestamp within that exact interval, and an ordinary zero exit. A record at or beyond the deadline refuses. A missing/partial/invalid record, failed clock, nonzero exit, signal or failed wait refuses. The successful record alone never authorizes output from a live or killed child.

At the existing deadline the parent still requests termination and reaps through its current cleanup path. If that path instead observes an ordinary zero exit and the complete timestamp proves success before the deadline, a previously set polling timeout flag alone does not turn the completed selection into a shadow failure. A child still executing at the deadline is terminated and cannot satisfy the ordinary-zero-exit requirement. I introduce no wait grace period and no larger execution budget. I do not claim that a blocking wait after SIGKILL bounds arbitrary kernel uninterruptible time; that is an existing supervision limitation, not a new sandbox guarantee.

I keep first-failure priority: clock failure and failed wait always refuse even if a record exists. The same precise existing deadline diagnostic applies to late completion and deadline termination. Other completion/exit failures preserve the existing publication refusal. My observer remains opt-in and cannot supply authority; the completion record is produced independently in ordinary mode.
