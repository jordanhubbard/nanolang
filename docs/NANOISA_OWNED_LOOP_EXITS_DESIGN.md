# I design structured borrowed loop exits

I own design-only investigation under #999 on `design/5.1-owned-loop-exits` from aggregate8f5b21af0. My unchanged anchors are `source_borrow_loop_exits.nano` and `source_borrow_range_for.nano`. I preserve their complete source, assertions, bounds evaluation, helper/caller ownership and successful cleanup. I first retain actual source-produced modules, original verification/VM, complete facts and current reconstruction refusal. I do not change admission in this design checkpoint.

I propose exact innermost structured break/continue outcomes for bounded natural pretest loops, including the existing single-exclusive helper signature. I distinguish return, break, continue and fallthrough states; complete reference regions and empty operand stacks are required at all abrupt edges. Continuing edges restore exact incoming owner origins. Break edges merge with the original false pretest exit, with initialized scalars intersected and no branch-local owner escape. Range bound expressions execute once in original left-to-right order before the new loop index binding; continue increments remain in their original bodies before the jump.

I require a separate helper termination/work proof: every continuing backedge must satisfy the same exact initialized rank and one nonoverflowing increment; break/return paths carry their actual work without an increment obligation. Nested caller loops are distinct from the currently nonnested helper rank proof. I preserve128 output locals, two functions, original verifier authority, no goto/dispatcher and all old-profile bytes. No output feasibility is claimed before measurement. The following baseline/contract sections will record actual transport and static accounting before production review.

## I retain the actual accepted source and transport

My [baseline](evidence/owned-loop-exits-baseline-20261019/README.md) records both unchanged fixtures through retained child NanoVirt and `nanoc_u8_context`. All four modules compile, pass original verification and execute with exit0. Both reconstruction targets atomically preserve prior output and refuse `one unconditional backedge per owned loop header`. Facts describe entry0 `()->INT`, helper1 `(exclusive Counter)->INT`, mode `[2]`, one exact nominal scalar-leaf Counter layout(INT,BOOL), and v1 local reference transport. Original entry/helper locals are14/2 for loop exits and31/3 for ranges. I propose the new profile only for this exact v1 single-exclusive family initially; other already admitted profiles retain their current gates and bytes.

The original C producer in `src/nanovirt/borrow_codegen.inc:1288` evaluates the range start and end left-to-right before storing end/index and opening the index name binding. `borrow_increment` at1269 is emitted before each range continue and the ordinary body fallthrough. Break emits a forward jump after original cleanup; continue targets the header after its own increment. I reconstruct these existing scalar statements and structured `while`, not a synthesized `for` that could evaluate bounds again, shadow an outer index early, or increment twice.

I preserve their exact forward conditional/return behavior; I do not claim an executable loop-backedge proof for optimized-away edges. The false break-only loop likewise may be forward-only. The false continue loop retains its backedge and is checked as such.

## I separate edge outcomes and state

I propose a new-profile `RegionResult` containing emitted statements, disjoint covered instruction positions, and explicit edge outcomes. Each edge carries kind(fallthrough,break,continue,return), exact target loop identity where relevant, scalar initialized set, live nominal/origin map, syntactic declaration availability and used-destination set. Return carries its exact scalar result and requires complete successful owned cleanup. Break/continue are not function returns. Operand stacks and reference state must be empty at every abrupt transfer; a complete borrow region is indivisible and cannot straddle an exit.

A loop descriptor records exact header/pretest/exit indices and the set of backedges. I admit only properly nested single-entry pretest intervals, unconditional continues to the innermost header and unconditional breaks to its exact exit. I reject crossing intervals, jumps into interiors, outer-loop transfers, conditional backedges, nonboundary targets and multiple entries. Every original instruction is covered once; forward joins cannot silently skip a trailing break/continue/return. Source branches that terminate both arms have no synthesized fallthrough.

At each branch I merge only fallthrough arms for subsequent statements, retaining abrupt outcomes separately. At a loop boundary I consume only this loop's break/continue outcomes. Every continue and normal backedge must restore the exact incoming owner origins and declarations; body-created owners must be consumed. Each break must also preserve that incoming surviving owner set for this bounded slice. Scalar initialization at the loop exit is the intersection of every reachable break state and the false-pretest state, including zero iterations; I do not strengthen initialization from the body. Pretest snapshot writes follow original instruction order on initial/final-false evaluation. Return exits propagate separately. Nested loop outcomes cannot contaminate parent backedge or post-loop states.

I reuse immutable exact origin identities from the reassignment proof, extending its control interface rather than replacing origin equality with nominal layout equality. The original proof IR remains untouched by rendering. The new profile does not add reassignment acceptance implicitly; composed assignments stay behind their existing proof/refusal boundaries until separately qualified.

C and Nano output use ordinary structured `break` and `continue`. In materialized-condition `while true` rendering, continue returns to the original condition snapshot region and break leaves the enclosing loop. No hidden dispatch, goto, interpreter or runtime iteration budget appears in reconstructed output.

## I prove helper work on every relevant exit

The helper checker must replace its terminal Boolean with separate outcome facts. It keeps immutable symbolic snapshots and merges equal facts only on continuing branch paths. Break must reach post-loop code, so it must trigger conservative invalidation of body-written locals; it cannot take the old return-only shortcut that skips after-loop invalidation. Actual function return remains terminal.

For rank loops I require dominating exact INT initialization and an exact strict `rank < bound` pretest. A bound may be a literal or an unchanged local snapshot whose symbolic value is an exact dominating literal, as in the actual range helper. The rank and bound cannot be invalidated on any continued path. On every normal/continue backedge, the symbolic rank must equal incoming rank+1 exactly once, with no stale preincrement snapshot used to write it twice. Break/return paths have no rank-update obligation; their actual work is still counted. I retain signed bounds proving no increment overflow on a continuing edge. Helper nested loops remain refused.

A body with only break/return outcomes has at most one executed iteration, not an arbitrary total-loop claim. A rank loop's conservative work bound includes the initial and final pretest snapshots, maximal body work across all exits on every possible iteration, and any after-loop/return work. The existing4096 logical proof-work limit and256 guarded-ADD charge remain unchanged. Unknown rank, dynamic helper bounds, wrong strides, unequal continuing updates, body writes to a bound, skipped increments and excessive work refuse atomically. I require complete original instruction coverage even in source-zero or structurally terminal forms.

## I retain exact arithmetic and two-function output

The exits caller contains one SUB; the range caller contains one DIV and15 ADDs. Existing scalar C helpers implement signed wrapping SUB and total signed DIV; `src/nanovm/vm.c` generic DIV and I64_DIV_S agree with `NANO_INTEGER_HELPERS['div']`: zero divisor returns0, MIN/-1 returnsMIN, other division truncates toward zero. The anchor's divisor is exact2, but I propose preserving the full existing guards when emitting a new-profile direct DIV statement, so broader operands cannot accidentally execute undefined native division.

New-profile Nano SUB may reuse the existing guarded explicit-statement expansion (seven syntactic locals), without changing the old multi-profile routing. DIV would snapshot left then right, retain low and an independent result (four locals), branch on zero and MIN/-1, and evaluate raw division only in the remaining safe arm. No helper function is added. Helper termination proof remains ADD-only; this caller arithmetic proposal does not admit DIV/SUB into rank proofs or condition whitelists. Endpoints, zero-divisor, signed truncation, once-only operands, assertion order and old-byte equality require actual paired tests before acceptance.

## I expose storage feasibility before implementation

`static-inventory.json` is an opcode inventory, not `nano_local_cost` on an admitted tree and not a regenerated count. With every LOAD/comparison conservatively materialized, existing seven-local ADD/SUB and proposed four-local DIV, the entry/helper inventory is101/23 for exits and255/25 for range. Pure pretests and existing exact folds can reduce it. However, the unoptimized range representation's27 original scalar slots plus15 guarded ADD expansions already total132, before ownership/observation/DIV storage. Its unchanged anchor cannot be credited by merely admitting CFG edges under that representation.

Hypothetical reuse of the already reviewed private ADD bank yields inventory94/16 and170/18 respectively, still not measured or sufficient proof of128 feasibility. I propose review of new-profile-only reuse of the existing finite original-slot rendering plan and private bank, after the untouched ownership/edge proof, rather than general register allocation or a higher cap. That plan must explicitly understand break/continue traversal and keep all prior disjoint coverage/dominance checks, snapshots and lasting ADD results. Any new rendering pattern requires separate review. I do not enable that reuse in this design commit.

The first implementation gate must regenerate both unchanged anchors and selected consistency shadows through ordinary CLI, compare exact estimated/actual entry/helper/shadow counts, and verify two-function admission. A diagnostic bypass can measure a refusal but cannot count as support. If128 fails, I preserve the whole fixture and stop for a representation decision; I do not trim assertions or substitute a smaller anchor.
