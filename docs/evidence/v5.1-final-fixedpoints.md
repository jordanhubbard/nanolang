# v5.1 compiler fixed points

I qualified both compiler execution routes from compiler-source pin
`43cbec85b29270e476fe54b75438317f8ee53aeb`. Each route compares raw
modules from its own successive compiler generations. I do not normalize
paths, rewrite imports, or omit the declared host closure.

The final release commit may follow this pin with interpreter, CI, roadmap,
and presentation changes. Those changes do not alter the compiler source used
by either retained fixed-point run.

## NanoVM route

- The initial module, Stage 1, and Stage 2 are each 850,160 bytes.
- All three have SHA-256
  `f20f5cc3bb9923a87ead6961f82f7427162210ad90e0e283d3264385a2a943bb`.
- Stage 1 generation took 1,043.367 seconds and Stage 2 took 1,041.867
  seconds under separate 1,200-second bounds.
- Both generated modules verified. Stage 2 compiled the unchanged hello
  source; the result verified and executed with `Hello from NanoLang!`.
- The exact five-library host closure remained equal across the initial,
  Stage 1, and Stage 2 modules. It contains `compiler_support`,
  `file_companion`, `file_source_catalog`, `nanoisa`, and `std`.
- The complete route took 2,502.483 seconds. Peak owned RSS was 18,680,456
  KiB during initial emission and 280,904 KiB during the two compared
  generations, below the 48 GiB bound.
- The retained console log has SHA-256
  `2680ca4448004cd95ba234b01d3c90aea32edb059849a702834076574939d60a`.
  Its manifest and post-run integrity record have SHA-256
  `059406c1d81f5164e56c45cce3db49adcbec70cb9092b36f71b61c616c8047d7`
  and
  `597bfd91fbd923d0d96c87e253e7e0dcbb29c4f92fda8ad8cf04837d577aced2`.

## Standalone native route

- The native Stage 1 and Stage 2 modules are each 850,160 bytes. Both have
  SHA-256
  `1aa25246e1153f5bdf575c926ce04aa391cf0a7fa418a8c87f015dda1137e356`.
- Stage 1 generation took 3,079.720 seconds and Stage 2 took 3,183.199
  seconds under separate 7,200-second bounds. Both modules verified.
- The exact five-library host closure remained equal across Stage 1 and Stage
  2. It contains `compiler_support`, `file_companion`,
  `file_source_catalog`, `nanoisa`, and `std`.
- I compiled the retained 24,348,878-byte initial C product with `-O1` in
  412.623 seconds. Stage 1 translation took 278.199 seconds, and its optimized
  native build took 409.872 seconds. The measured route took 7,365.986
  seconds. Peak owned RSS was 6,528,720 KiB, below the 48 GiB bound.
- The Stage 1 native compiler compiled the unchanged hello source; the result
  verified and executed with `Hello from NanoLang!`. Neither native compiler
  links `nano_vm`.
- The VM route's initial module has the same 850,160-byte size but different
  raw bytes. Its dump differs only in absolute private-library paths created
  before the native route refreshed those artifacts. I did not normalize
  either module. The native Stage 1 and Stage 2 dumps are byte-identical.
- The retained manifest and post-run integrity record have SHA-256
  `69e42e6966be28fb8509e02a4fbfd996c5c84991a61d618e987013ab681139c4`
  and
  `a50864f1c8b451fb0bece1b7cf577915a741c88a710006a477121cae1d3c6cf3`.

These fixed points establish reproducible compiler artifacts for the tested
routes and host closure. They do not prove compiler correctness.
